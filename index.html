      AirSaathi - built for PromptWars Virtual, Challenge 06: Urban Air Quality Assistant.

      Air-quality data: Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS / satellite and monitor based model, values are estimates, hourly model resolution; the global dot layer is one batched request, cached for 3 hours on this device). Place names: BigDataCloud reverse geocoder. AQI shown on the US EPA scale. Emission savings are labelled estimates, not measurements. Health guidance follows US EPA AQI action tables (EPA-456/F-14-002) and is not medical advice - follow your doctor's plan for asthma or heart conditions.

      AI assistant settings

      The free default sends your question, the current city AQI snapshot and a few saved profile facts to Pollinations.ai, a third-party service - it leaves this device. Pick Browser AI to keep everything on-device, or use your own Gemini key (stored only in this browser).
